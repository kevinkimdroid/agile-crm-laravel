1. Getting started

Sign in

  1. Open the CRM in your browser.
  2. Enter your username (or email) and password.
  3. Click Sign in.

You land on the Dashboard.

Forgot password

  1. On the sign-in page, click Forgot password.
  2. Enter the email on your user account.
  3. Open the reset link sent to that inbox and choose a new password.

If you do not receive the email, ask an administrator to send a reset link from Settings → Users.

Sign out

Click your name (top right) → Log out.

What you can see

Menus and buttons follow your role and profile.

RoleTypical access
Customer service / supportClients, tickets, FAQ, maturities, complaints
Sales / marketingProspects, leads, campaigns, social, WhatsApp, broadcast
FinanceCheques, agency advances, receipt reprint
AdministratorSettings, users, SLA, modules, all reports

If you need a screen that is hidden, ask an administrator to update your profile.

On a phone or small screen, tap the menu icon (top left) to open the sidebar.

Top bar

  • Search — type a name, email, phone, policy, or ticket keyword. Results appear as you type.
  • Quick Create — add a Lead, Opportunity, Ticket, Work Ticket, Event, or Task without leaving the page.
  • Reports and Tickets shortcuts.
  • Your name — Profile, Settings, Log out.

Prospects vs clients

  • Prospects are people in the CRM who are not yet (or not only) policyholders — sales contacts.
  • Clients are policyholders from the insurance systems (Group Life, Individual Life, Mortgage, Group Pension, and related lines).

Use Serve Client when a caller is already a policyholder. Use Prospects for sales follow-up.

3. Dashboard

The dashboard is your home screen. It shows pipeline value and active deals; prospect, lead, and client counts; ticket volumes (Open, In Progress, Awaiting, Closed); and shortcuts for insurance operations (Clients, Group Life, Individual, New Ticket, Maturities).

Typical start of day

  1. Check open and overdue tickets.
  2. Open Clients or Serve Client if you take calls.
  3. Use New Ticket when a customer needs a logged issue.

4. Prospects

Sidebar → Prospects

Find a prospect

Search by name, email, phone, or mobile. Click a name or Open to view the record.

Add a prospect

  1. Click Add Prospect.
  2. Enter name and contact details.
  3. Save.

Prospect record

The profile shows phone, email, and tabs such as Summary, Details, Tickets, Updates/comments, Campaigns, Calendar, and Calls/SMS/emails where available.

Useful actions: Edit contact details; add a follow-up; add a comment; add or remove the prospect from a campaign; click the phone number to call (uses the office PBX if it is set up for you).

5. Clients

Sidebar → Clients

Clients come from the insurance / ERP systems. You can filter by line of business (for example Group Life or Individual).

Search and open a client

  1. Go to Clients.
  2. Search by name, policy number, phone, or ID as available.
  3. Open the client.

Serve Client (fast path for the phone)

Support → Serve Client (also linked from the Support hub).

  1. Search the client.
  2. Confirm the policy and product.
  3. Create a ticket from that screen if needed.

Use this when you already have a policy number or a caller who is a known client.

Client record

Header shows name, phone, line of business, policy number, and consent status.

Common actions: create a ticket from the client or a related policy; email the client; record consent; add a comment; upload a document; send an M-Pesa payment prompt from the Premiums tab if M-Pesa is enabled.

Create or import clients

If your role allows it: Create Client adds a record in CRM; Import uses a downloadable template. Skipped rows are listed after import. Most live policy data still comes from ERP — treat CRM create/import as the exception unless your team has a defined process.

6. Tickets

Tickets are customer issues in the help desk. Sidebar → Tickets (or the ticket icon in the top bar).

Statuses

StatusMeaning
OpenNew or not yet being worked
In ProgressSomeone is working it
Wait For Response (Awaiting)Waiting on the customer or another party
ClosedResolved
InactiveParked; hidden from active lists
UnassignedNo owner (shown when any exist)

Filter with the status pills at the top of the list. Search with Ctrl+K or the search box (ticket text, prospect, assigned to / by).

Create a ticket

From New Ticket, Quick Create → Ticket, a client, Serve Client, Mail Manager, or a finance cheque.

  1. Enter a title.
  2. Choose Assigned To.
  3. Link a prospect or client (required). Policy number fills when the record has one.
  4. Choose category (drives SLA / TAT).
  5. Optionally add description, source, product line, and severity.
  6. Optionally add a custom message for the client notification email.
  7. Save.

Default priority is Normal unless your form is set otherwise.

Work a ticket

Open the ticket. You will see status, priority, category, policy, and ticket number.

  • Comment — keep the trail on the ticket.
  • Reassign — send to another user or group. History appears on the ticket.
  • Knowledge Base tab — search FAQs, click Use as solution, then close.
  • Close Ticket — enter a short resolution (or leave blank). Closing can trigger a feedback email/SMS to the customer.
  • Inactivate — remove from active lists without closing (confirm when prompted).
  • Edit — change fields if you have permission.
  • Jump to the linked Client or Prospect.

Recommended close flow

  1. Open the ticket.
  2. Open the Knowledge Base tab.
  3. Search FAQs → Use as solution.
  4. Click Close Ticket and confirm the solution text.

Use Export on the tickets list when you need a spreadsheet of the current view.

7. FAQ and knowledge base

Sidebar → FAQ & Categories

Staff use FAQs to answer tickets consistently: browse or search articles, mark an article helpful, and from a ticket search FAQs and Use as solution.

Users who manage content can create and edit categories and articles. Keep titles short and answers specific so agents can find them while the customer is on the line.

8. Maturities and renewals

Maturities

Support → Maturities

  • Lists policies approaching maturity.
  • Update renewal status.
  • Notify client (looks up contact details first).
  • Generate or email a discharge voucher (PDF).
  • Export the list.

Investment maturities

Support → Investment maturities

  • Policies maturing in the next 14 days (default window).
  • Shows whether a notification was already sent.
  • Send one notification email per policy; sent rows are tracked so you do not double-send.

Renewals (mortgage)

Support → Renewals

Renewal dates due in the coming period (default 30 days; change the period on the page). Export for follow-up lists.

9. Complaint register

Support → Complaint Register

Used for IRA-style complaint tracking. Complaints can also be created from inbound mail when automation is enabled.

Register a complaint

  1. Click to create / register a complaint.
  2. Optionally look up a client / policy (fills name, policy, email, phone).
  3. Optionally link a prospect.
  4. Enter date received (required) and source (Phone, Email, In person, Written letter, IRA referral, Other).
  5. Complete complainant details and the complaint description.
  6. Save.

Case status: Received, Under Investigation, Pending Response, Resolved, Escalated to IRA, Closed.

Register classification: Complaint, Needs review, Not a complaint.

Update register status when a logged item is not a formal complaint, or when it needs review. Use Export for compliance reporting.

10. Sales: leads, deals, campaigns

Leads

Sales → Leads (or sidebar Add Lead)

  1. Click Add Lead.
  2. Enter name, contact, source, and status.
  3. Save and follow up from the lead record.

Filter by status and search from the list. Leads can also be created from a social media interaction.

Deals (opportunities)

Create from Quick Create → Opportunity or Deals. Track stage and value. Pipeline value on the dashboard comes from active deals.

Campaigns

Sales → Campaigns

Create campaigns, then add prospects from a prospect record (Campaigns tab) or from campaign tools. Use campaigns together with Broadcast when you need a mail or SMS drop.

11. Marketing: social, WhatsApp, broadcast

Social media

Sales → Social Media

  • Review inbound interactions.
  • Schedule a post or Publish now.
  • Cancel a scheduled post.
  • Create lead from an interaction.
  • Connect or disconnect Facebook / other platforms via Social auth if your account is allowed.

WhatsApp

Sales → WhatsApp

Company WhatsApp inbox: read conversations and send replies. The same chat may also appear under Support, depending on your menu.

Email & SMS broadcast

Sales → Broadcast (also under Support for some roles)

  1. Filter the audience (client type, search, skip recently emailed/SMS’d if offered).
  2. Compose the message or pick an email / SMS template.
  3. Select recipients (list, or upload using the recipients template).
  4. Send.

You can create broadcast templates from this screen. Check Bounced Emails under Reports after large sends.

12. Finance

Finance screens are restricted to Finance department users and Administrators.

Finance cheques

Finance → Finance cheques

Live cheque feed from FMS. Filter by system source and other fields, then create a ticket from a cheque when a payment needs follow-up.

Agency advances

Finance → Agency advances

Same idea for agency advance records from FMS.

Receipt reprint

Finance → Receipt reprint

  1. Choose search type: Receipt No., Policy No., or Client Name.
  2. Enter the value (internal number, branch receipt code, policy, or name).
  3. Open a result to preview.
  4. Reprint a sealed duplicate, or download XML if required.

If the receipt service is down, the page shows an error — contact IT rather than retrying endlessly.

13. Credit Life statements

Statements → Credit Life Statements

Select the period / recipients as shown on the page and send statements. Use this only if your team owns Credit Life communication.

14. Work tickets

Work Management → Work Tickets

Internal work tracking (not the customer help desk). Useful for remote / daily work with manager visibility.

  • My Tickets — assigned to you.
  • Team Activity — your team (managers).
  • All Tickets — organisation-wide (admins / senior managers).

Statuses: Open, In Progress, Blocked, Closed, Cancelled. The list also shows TAT breached counts.

  1. New Work Ticket (or Quick Create).
  2. Fill title, description, assignee, priority.
  3. Open a ticket to post updates.
  4. Reassign when ownership changes.

Managers configure reporting lines from Setup / Users so Team Activity is accurate. Export is available from the list.

15. Calendar

Tools → Calendar

Create Events and Tasks from the calendar or Quick Create. List and edit activities; filter overdue from the dashboard menu; link activities to contacts where the form allows it.

This calendar is the CRM activity list. It is not a full replacement for a personal Outlook calendar.

16. Reports

Tools → Reports (or the chart icon in the top bar)

Audit & compliance: Client Tickets – Broken SLA; Work Tickets – Broken SLA; Ticket Aging (open tickets older than 7+ days); Tickets by Date; Ticket Audit Trail (reassignments); Assignment Handlers; Bounced Emails.

Performance & workload: Usage & Top Issues; Ticket Workload; Automation Analysis; Work Activities.

CRM & operations: Prospects Summary; Calls Summary; Deals Pipeline / Leads (links into those modules).

Most reports support Excel export; some support PDF. Use Export all (Excel) or Export all (PDF) from the hub when you need a pack. Set date from / date to before exporting so the file matches the period you intend to review.

17. Tools

PBX & Calls

Tools → PBX & Calls

  • See recent calls and recordings (where stored).
  • Make a call (click-to-call) if your extension is mapped.
  • Claim an unassigned incoming call so it sits on your user.
  • Live view for current call activity.

Clicking a phone number on a client or prospect uses this integration when your extension is mapped in Settings.

Mail Manager

Tools → Mail Manager

Shared company mailbox (Microsoft 365 or IMAP, depending on setup).

  • Fetch Emails if the inbox looks stale (health banner shows last successful fetch).
  • Open an email.
  • Create ticket from email when the message is a customer issue.
  • Create Email to send from the shared mailbox.

Tickets and complaints may also be created automatically from inbound mail when automation is on.

Email templates

Tools → Email Templates

Create and edit templates used by tickets, broadcast, and other sends. Keep merge fields intact; do not delete placeholders you do not understand.

ERP Messaging

Tools → ERP Messaging

Send messages related to ERP client data and review the Sent log.

Protect PDF

Tools → Protect PDF

Upload a PDF and apply password protection before sending to a client. PDF Maker (template layout for modules) is available to users with PDF Maker access, usually via Tools.

19. Customer feedback

When a support ticket is closed, the customer may receive a link to a public form. They choose whether they were happy or not satisfied, may add comments, then submit and see a thank-you page.

You do not log in to fill this form. Staff should not complete it on the customer’s behalf.

20. Tips and troubleshooting

SituationWhat to do
Menu item missingYou likely lack permission. Ask an admin to adjust your profile.
Cannot open FinanceFinance is limited to Finance department and Administrators.
Search finds nothingTry policy number, phone, or a shorter name. Use Serve Client for policyholders.
Mail looks oldOpen Mail Manager and click Fetch Emails. If health stays stale, tell IT.
Click-to-call failsConfirm your PBX extension is mapped and you are logged in to the phone.
Receipt / cheque errorERP/FMS may be unavailable. Note the message and contact IT.
Duplicate client emailsCheck consent and existing tickets before creating another ticket.
Ticket SLA red / brokenWork or close it, or reassign. Managers use Broken SLA reports.
Forgot password email missingCheck spam; ask an admin to send a reset link.
Page looks wrong on phoneUse the menu icon; prefer landscape for tables and reports.

Do

  • Search for an existing client or ticket before creating a new one.
  • Write ticket titles that another agent can understand.
  • Close tickets with a real solution (FAQ text is preferred).
  • Treat client documents, receipts, and IDs as confidential.

Do not

  • Share your password.
  • Close tickets without a note when the customer is still waiting.
  • Send broadcasts without checking the audience filters.
  • Reprint receipts unless you are in Finance (or an admin covering that role).
