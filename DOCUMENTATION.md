# Agile Craft CRM — User Guide

This guide covers the core, day-to-day workflows: signing in, the dashboard, and working with Contacts, Leads, and Support Tickets. It does not cover admin/settings, finance, PBX, or marketing modules.

> **Note:** Screenshots are not yet included in this guide — they'll be added once we can walk through the live application together. Menu labels and field names below are based on the app's routes and should be checked against the actual screens and adjusted if wording differs.

## 1. Signing In

1. Open the CRM in your browser at the address provided by your administrator.
2. Enter your **username** and **password** on the login screen.
3. If you've forgotten your password, use the "Forgot password" link to request a reset.
4. After signing in, you'll land on your **Dashboard**.

## 2. The Dashboard

The dashboard is your home screen after login. It shows key metrics and activity at a glance — things like open tickets, recent leads, and other stats relevant to your role. Some dashboard statistics update in real time as records change.

Use the search bar (usually at the top of the screen) to quickly find contacts, leads, or other records by name or keyword from anywhere in the app.

## 3. Working with Contacts

Contacts are the people/organizations already known to the CRM.

- **View contacts**: Go to the **Contacts** section from the main menu to see the full list.
- **Search/filter**: Use the search bar or list filters to narrow down to the contact you need.
- **View a contact's details**: Click a contact to open their record — you'll typically see their details, related activity, and any follow-ups or comments logged against them.
- **Add a follow-up or comment**: Open a contact's record and use the follow-up/comment option to log notes about your interaction with them.

## 4. Working with Leads

Leads are prospective contacts that haven't yet been converted.

- **View leads**: Go to the **Leads** section from the main menu.
- **Add a new lead**: Use the "New Lead" option and fill in the prospect's details.
- **Convert a lead**: Once a lead is qualified, use the convert option to turn it into a Contact (and, where applicable, a Deal).

## 5. Working with Support Tickets

Tickets track customer support requests through to resolution.

1. **View tickets**: Go to **Support > Tickets** to see your ticket queue.
2. **Create a ticket**: Use "New Ticket," fill in the customer/contact, category, source, and description, and save.
3. **Update a ticket**: Open a ticket to add comments, change its status, or reassign it to a different team member. A history of reassignments is kept on the ticket.
4. **Close a ticket**: Once resolved, close the ticket. Closing a ticket may trigger a feedback request to the customer, asking them to rate their experience via an emailed link.
5. **SLA tracking**: Tickets are tracked against service-level targets; tickets nearing or past their SLA deadline may be flagged or trigger reminders to the assigned agent.

## 6. Getting Help

If you run into an issue not covered here, or need access to a module not described in this guide (Finance, Marketing/Social Media, PBX/Calls, or Admin/Settings), contact your system administrator.
